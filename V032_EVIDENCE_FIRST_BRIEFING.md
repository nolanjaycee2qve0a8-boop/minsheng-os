# v0.32 — Evidence-First Research Briefing

Briefings are assembled from immutable structured statements, then rendered by controlled templates. The engine is offline and deterministic; it neither calls an external model nor acquires data.

Every numeric statement retains `sourceRecordIds`; REAL statements retain evidence locators; DERIVED statements retain formula and inputs. Forecast, scenario, proxy/sample, UNKNOWN/BLOCKED and v0.31 pending-review statements remain distinct. Pending review is excluded from formal headlines.

Current seed includes five acceptance briefings: official-change digest, sector status, data gaps, forecast outcome and combined research briefing. The change digest reports `NO_NEW_QUALIFIED_RELEASE`; it does not invent a release from the v0.31 review queue.
